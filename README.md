### 🧭 About me

I'm the person who says "I'll just build a quick system for that" and then disappears for two weeks into RFID scanners, MySQL schemas, and admin dashboards. I split my time between two worlds that don't usually mix: **routing packets** and **routing HTTP requests**. Cisco-certified on one side, PHP/MySQL on the other — I like knowing how the whole stack works, from the switch to the `<div>`.

Currently studying **BS Information Technology** at Eastern Samar State University – Guiuan Campus, and building real systems for my College of Computer Studies along the way (because tutorials are fine, but production bugs teach faster).

- 🔭 Recently shipped: RFID attendance systems, fines monitoring, and campus e-logbooks
- 🌱 Currently leveling up: cybersecurity + backend architecture
- 💬 Ask me about: Cisco networking, PHP/MySQL systems, or why RFID debouncing is harder than it sounds
